MY STORY

Good hair starts with feeling heard.

Hi, I'm Emily — a passionate salon owner and experienced stylist who believes that great hair is about more than how it looks. It's about how you feel when you walk out the door.

At Dusk Till Dawn, my goal is to create a welcoming space where every client feels comfortable, listened to and cared for. I take the time to understand your style, your routine and what makes you feel most like yourself.

Whether you're ready for a fresh cut, a colour update or simply a little change, we'll work together to find a look that feels right for you.
